## English

An ongoing project converting historical Chinese texts into searchable, source-linked EPUBs. **9/21** currently catalogued books or thematic collections have an initial digital text; full collation remains in progress. Letters and short pieces are grouped rather than counted individually. This is a dated project inventory, not a definitive total of every surviving work.

The sample contains one complete 1915 essay, eight source images, navigation and explicit uncertainty notes. My work covers text organization, image-based checking, revision tracking and EPUB production.
